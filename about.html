About DSARPro

We are on a mission to simplify data privacy and empower organizations to build trust through transparent data practices.

Our Story

Founded on Privacy First Principles

In 2018, when GDPR was first enacted, companies struggled to manage the overwhelming influx of Data Subject Access Requests (DSAR). Manual processes, spreadsheets, and emails were not only inefficient but highly insecure.

DSARPro was founded by a team of cybersecurity experts and legal professionals who recognized the need for a purpose-built automation tool. We envisioned a world where compliance doesn't hinder business growth, but rather acts as a competitive advantage.

Our Mission

To provide accessible, automated, and hyper-secure tools that help global enterprises respect user privacy rights without the operational overhead.

Our Vision

A digital ecosystem where data privacy is implicitly guaranteed, and organizations can seamlessly demonstrate accountability and transparency.
