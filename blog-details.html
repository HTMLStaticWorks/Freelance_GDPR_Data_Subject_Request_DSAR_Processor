The Real Cost of Manual DSAR Processing

By Michael Chang • Oct 12, 2025 • 5 min read

Processing Data Subject Access Requests (DSARs) is a fundamental requirement under the GDPR. However, many organizations still rely on manual processes—spreadsheets, emails, and IT ticket requests—to handle them. As privacy awareness grows and the volume of requests increases, the hidden costs of this manual approach are becoming unsustainable.

The Labor Intensive Nature of Discovery

The most time-consuming part of fulfilling a DSAR is locating the data. On average, a mid-sized enterprise uses over 130 SaaS applications. When a request comes in, IT and compliance teams must manually search through these disjointed systems. Industry studies suggest that a single manual DSAR can cost upwards of $1,500 in labor alone.

The Risk of Human Error

Beyond financial cost, manual processing introduces significant risk. When human operators are tasked with sifting through gigabytes of data and redacting third-party PII, mistakes happen. A missed redaction isn't just an oops—it's a data breach, carrying potential fines of up to 4% of global revenue.

"Automation is no longer a luxury in data privacy; it is a fundamental requirement for maintaining compliance at scale without halting business operations."

The Path Forward

By implementing an automated DSAR solution, organizations can reduce processing time by up to 80%, ensure accurate and comprehensive data discovery, and eliminate the risk of accidental exposure. In the modern regulatory landscape, investing in privacy technology yields an immediate ROI in risk mitigation and operational efficiency.
